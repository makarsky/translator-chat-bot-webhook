Useful translation tools, with fewer steps between you and the conversation

01 / FIND YOUR LANGUAGE

Your recent languages, right up front

Translate messages into any of 105 languages. Seven recently used languages stay on the first page, so your regular conversations are always quick to reach.

02 / LISTEN ALONG

Read it or hear it

Forward a message from another chat to translate it, then use text-to-speech for translations up to 200 characters. Speech availability depends on the language.

03 / LESS TAPPING

Automatic switching between two languages

No need to manually swap your two most recently used languages. The bot switches between them as you translate.
